# Quill & Ink Basic and Premium Access

## Purpose
This document explains the difference between basic-free and Premium access in Quill & Ink.

## Basic-Free Access
Plugins classified as `basic-free` are available without purchasing Quill & Ink Premium.

Basic-free access does not necessarily mean every command works for every member without restrictions. Individual plugins may require specific Discord permissions, configuration, role requirements, or other documented prerequisites.

Use the approved plugin files and plugin index to determine which plugins are basic-free. Do not assume a plugin is free based on its name.

## Premium Access
Plugins marked `premium: true` require the applicable Quill & Ink Premium entitlement.

Premium provides access to Premium-gated plugins and their related experiences, subject to the requirements documented for each feature.

Do not assume that Premium removes Discord permission requirements or automatically configures every plugin.

## How to Determine a Plugin's Access Level
Use the approved plugin information as the source of truth:

- `premium: true` means the plugin requires Premium.
- Plugins without `premium: true` are classified as basic-free, according to the project's plugin classification rule.

Do not invent or infer access levels from a plugin's name. When answering questions about a specific plugin, check its approved knowledge file and the plugin index.

## Premium Price and Duration
Quill & Ink Premium costs ₹350 / around $4 for 30 days.

The subscription does not automatically renew. Users should follow the purchase flow provided by the bot for the current purchase instructions.

Do not promise a refund, extension, discount, or special entitlement unless the relevant approved policy or documentation confirms it.

## How to Purchase Premium
Premium purchases are initiated through the Discord bot, not directly through the website.

1. Run `/setup` in the Discord server.
2. Select the Premium option.
3. Follow the instructions provided by the bot to continue the purchase process.

Do not invent payment steps, checkout links, or payment methods. Refer users to the bot's current purchase flow or the official support server if they encounter a problem.

## Troubleshooting Premium Access
If a user has purchased Premium but cannot access a Premium-gated feature:

1. Identify the affected server and plugin.
2. Check the plugin's approved Premium classification and requirements.
3. Check the relevant server configuration and Discord permissions.
4. If the problem remains unresolved, direct the user to the official support server.

Do not claim that a payment succeeded or that Premium has been activated unless there is evidence confirming it.
