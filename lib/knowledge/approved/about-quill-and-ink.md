# About Quill & Ink

## What Is Quill & Ink?

Quill & Ink is a multipurpose Discord bot designed to help communities manage their servers, give members more ways to participate, and create engaging shared experiences.

It brings different community tools together through a plugin-based system. Instead of needing a separate bot for every task, server owners can explore Quill & Ink's available plugins and use the features that suit their community.

Quill & Ink's philosophy is simple: **Built for communities. Made for stories.**

## Why Add Quill & Ink?

Communities need different things. Some need moderation tools to keep conversations organized, while others want games, an economy, voice-channel conveniences, welcome messages, and other ways to engage members.

Quill & Ink brings these capabilities together in one bot, with a range of plugins covering community management, entertainment, utility, and engagement.

Users should consider adding Quill & Ink when they want to:
- Bring multiple community tools together in one Discord bot.
- Manage and moderate their server with the relevant available plugins.
- Give members interactive activities and engagement features.
- Explore additional tools as their community grows.
- Choose the features that best fit their server's needs.

Available features and access requirements depend on the individual plugin and the server's setup.

## What Can Quill & Ink Do?

Quill & Ink provides functionality through plugins. Its documented plugin categories include:

- **Moderation and safety:** Tools for managing server interactions and moderation tasks.
- **Community and welcome features:** Welcome messages and other community-focused features.
- **Economy:** Virtual currency and related economy features.
- **Games and entertainment:** Interactive activities for server members.
- **Voice:** Temporary voice-channel functionality.
- **Utility and information:** Commands that display user, bot, server, bank, and network information.
- **Additional community tools:** Other plugins documented in the approved plugin knowledge.

Each plugin has its own commands, configuration, requirements, and behaviour. Consult its approved knowledge file for exact instructions instead of assuming every feature works the same way.

## Free Plugins and Premium Plugins

Quill & Ink offers basic plugins as well as Premium-gated plugins.

**Plugin classification rule:** If the approved plugin file contains `premium: true`, the plugin requires Premium. If the approved plugin file does not contain `premium: true`, treat it as a basic plugin for access questions.

Do not infer Premium requirements from a plugin's name, complexity, or missing source-code details. Apply the approved-file rule consistently.

A basic plugin does not require the Premium entitlement solely to access that plugin. Its individual commands may still have their own permissions, configuration requirements, or other restrictions.

Premium unlocks access to Premium plugins and experiences. It does not mean that every feature in the bot is Premium-only.

## Quill & Ink Premium

Premium is an optional purchase for communities that want access to additional Quill & Ink experiences.

**Price:** ₹350 / around $4 for 30 days.

Premium includes:
- Access to Premium plugins and experiences.
- 30 days of Premium access.
- No automatic renewal.

When the 30-day period ends, users can choose whether to purchase Premium again. It does not renew automatically.

### How to Purchase Premium

Premium is purchased through Quill & Ink in Discord, not directly through the website.

1. Add Quill & Ink to your Discord server if it is not already there.
2. Run `/setup` in your server.
3. Select the Premium option in the setup panel.
4. Follow the purchase steps provided by the bot.

The purchase flow and options presented by the bot are the authoritative guide during checkout.
